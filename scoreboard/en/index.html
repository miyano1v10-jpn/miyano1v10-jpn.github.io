Basics

  • Tap a score panel to add a point; swipe down to remove one
  • Undo reverses the last scoring action (including the end of a set or match)
  • Tap a team name to rename it
  • Set endings, deuce, the final set, and serve are handled automatically

FAQ

Q. How do I connect two devices?

On the device you want to use as the scoreboard, choose “Use as Display” on the Settings screen. On the device you will hold, choose “Use as Remote”, then pick the scoreboard device from the list. Any combination of iPhone and iPad works, and no internet connection is needed.

Q. The other device doesn’t show up in Two-Device Mode

  • Turn on Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on both devices (they don’t need to be joined to a Wi-Fi network).
  • In iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network, make sure the App is turned on.
  • Keep both devices close together with the App open.
  • If it still doesn’t appear, quit the App on both devices and try again.

Q. Two-Device Mode disconnected during a match

The match is kept on the remote device. The App reconnects automatically, so please wait a moment. If it doesn’t reconnect, tap Reconnect, or switch back to a single device and keep going.

Q. What happens to the match if I close the App?

The match is saved automatically and picks up where you left off the next time you open the App. To start over, tap New match.

Q. Can I change the rules during a match?

Yes, on the Settings screen. Under “If changed mid-match”, choose whether the change applies from the next set or to the current set too.

Q. How do I remove ads?

Buy “Remove Ads” (a one-time purchase) on the Settings screen to hide banner ads and post-match ads. In Two-Device Mode, if either device has purchased it, ads are hidden on both devices.

Q. Ads came back after I changed phones or reinstalled the App

Tap “Restore Purchase” on the Settings screen. If you bought it with the same Apple ID, it is restored for free.

Q. My purchase isn’t reflected

Restart the App somewhere with a good connection and tap “Restore Purchase” on the Settings screen. If that doesn’t help, contact us with the date and time of purchase. Refund requests are handled by Apple (Report a Problem).
